# register-guide.md: choosing and holding a register

Register is the first decision in Phase 1. It sets diction, sentence length, how colloquial the
dialogue gets, and how much you may domesticate. Name the register from the source, then keep it.

## The register spectrum (pick by source type)

| Register | When | What it means in Persian |
|---|---|---|
| **Modern-natural** (default) | most contemporary prose, articles, fiction | current everyday diction; short-to-medium sentences; no archaism, no over-Arabic phrasing, no officialese |
| **Casual / dialogue** | spoken lines, informal narration, child POV | spoken forms («می‌گه», «کتاب‌هاتو»), colloquial markers, تو/شما by relationship, not by dictionary |
| **Literary / elevated** | literary or modernist sources | richer diction, longer periods, more figurative language. Only when the *source* is literary; don't ornament neutral text |
| **Classic / formal** | period pieces, classic-style writing | older, more formal diction and measured cadence |
| **Expository / nonfiction** | essays, technical, reference, science | precise, clear, neutral; consistent terminology; no ornament, and no bureaucratic verbs (see `ai-tells.md` §1) |
| **Official / legal** | contracts, regulations, letters to institutions | formal is correct here, but still «است» not «می‌باشد»; the formality comes from vocabulary and structure, not from helper verbs |
| **Children's** | kids' books | simple syntax, concrete vocabulary, playful tone |

## Spoken vs written Persian in dialogue
Persian has a written standard and a spoken standard (گفتاری). Contemporary fiction usually writes
dialogue in the spoken form: «می‌خوام برم» not «می‌خواهم بروم», «کتابا» or «کتاب‌ها» by taste,
«اون» for «آن». Narration stays written. Pick one convention for a text and hold it; a line that
switches mid-dialogue is a tell. Match the source: a formal speaker in English stays formal in
Persian.

## Rules for holding register
- **Match the speaker, not just the text.** Narration may be formal while dialogue is colloquial
  (P8, P28). Don't flatten a casual line into bookish Persian.
- **Don't let "literary" leak into plain text.** Elevated diction on a neutral source is itself a
  machine-Persian tell.
- **Don't let "official" leak into anything.** «می‌باشد», «مورد … قرار می‌گیرد», «دارای» are not
  formality; they are officialese, and models over-produce them.
- **Keep formality consistent** within a passage; switch only when the source switches.
- **Modern-natural is the safe default** when the register is unclear.
